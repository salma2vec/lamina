      Help

      Section cuts the specimen with a plane. Cane turns the section into coloured clays. Pattern lays slices of the cane on a slab or a bowl.

      Drag to turn. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Double-click to frame the object. In Section, drag the plane to move it.

      The flat view shows the section or cane front-on, with a scale bar. Drag there to move the section under the outline; scroll to zoom it.

      Cane templates draw a constructed cane from a few measurements instead of a scan. The build sheet lists the parts and the steps to assemble them.

      Today is a daily study: the same specimen and plane for everyone on a date. Look at the section, sketch a cane with the given number of clays, and stop when the timer ends. Reveal shows Lamina's cane.
